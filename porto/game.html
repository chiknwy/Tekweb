The Leak's Nights

The Leak’s Nights is a first-person horror game themed around Balinese culture, set in a village preparing for a performance of Calonarang. The player takes on the role of a university student who has returned home and accidentally triggers a series of supernatural events related to the Leak, a shadow creature that haunts those who disrespect tradition. The player's objective is to uncover the village's secrets, solve ritualistic puzzles, and ultimately light sacred incense to stop the curse. Gameplay lasts for approximately 1–2 hours, with a focus on atmosphere, exploration, and environmental storytelling.
