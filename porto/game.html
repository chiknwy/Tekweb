The Leak's Nights

A first-person horror game rooted in Balinese culture. Set in a village preparing for a Calonarang performance, you play a university student who accidentally awakens the Leak — a shadow creature that haunts those who disrespect tradition. Uncover secrets, solve ritualistic puzzles, and light sacred incense to break the curse.

~1–2 hours gameplay First-Person Horror Roblox Studio Balinese Culture
